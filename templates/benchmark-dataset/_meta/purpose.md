# Purpose

## Mission
Hold one benchmark dataset's documents exactly as published, so a search over them can be scored against the
relevance judgments people made for the dataset's queries. Every document in this knowledge base is digested. A
digest captures what the document claims and how it shows it, whatever the document's subject.

## In scope
Every document of the dataset, mirrored one per source, and one digest page per document. Every finding or method a
document states bears on this purpose, so no document is skipped for its subject.

## Out of scope
Any document from outside the dataset, and any edit to a mirrored document.

## Consumers
`studio evals score`, and the generator check that compares generated datasets with the human-judged one.
