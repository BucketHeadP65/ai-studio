# Compounding policy

## digest-sources
One digest per mirror, typed by the mirror's item type through sources.digest_types. Read the source whole, claim only what a quote supports, split a long source into a lead page plus section pages.
A code-repository README is digested only when it yields at least one finding that is not an author-claim (a benchmark result, an ablation, or an observation) bearing on the base's purpose. Otherwise write it as status skipped with a summary that starts with Skipped: and names the reason, the way an empty mirror is skipped, never as an active digest.

## flag-ideas
Read the new digests for ideas worth the owner's attention, grounded in the base's purpose and its flag history. A flag that restates a digest or names no evidence is not a flag. A defect in our own page is repaired, never flagged.
